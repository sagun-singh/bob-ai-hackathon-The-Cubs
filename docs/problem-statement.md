# Problem Statement

## Background

[Law enforcement agencies generate massive text volumes through digitized FIR repositories like India’s CCTNS, which hold crores of records without NLP capabilities. These siloed databases prevent inter district tracking, allowing sophisticated syndicates to evade detection. Because crime analysis remains entirely manual, agencies need an AI-driven tool to automatically ingest FIRs, extract entities, and surface hidden repeat offender patterns.]

## The Problem

[Law enforcement investigators waste hours manually cross-referencing thousands of unstructured FIRs because legacy systems like CCTNS lack automated NLP layers. Consequently, investigators miss crucial inter-district connections, allowing serial offenders operating across jurisdictional boundaries to evade detection for years.]


## Who is Affected

[Local police station officers, crime branch analysts, and detectives who have to manually read and connect thousands of separate police reports across different towns and districts.]

## Why It Matters

[This manual process costs precious time during active investigations and introduces severe public safety risks. When cross-district patterns are missed, dangerous serial offenders and cyber-crime syndicates remain free to commit more crimes, delaying justice for victims and leaving communities vulnerable to preventable repeat offenses.]

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

[Currently, when crimes occur across different districts, it is extremely difficult to manually spot patterns between similar cases, making the entire investigation process painfully slow. We need this application to automatically uncover these hidden cross-district connections. By linking related cases, law enforcement can solve crimes faster, put repeat offenders behind bars, and ensure victims receive timely justice and compensation.]
